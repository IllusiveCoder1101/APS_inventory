Here's the complete context for the app:

The app we are building is a inventory management app, we are trying to handle four types of items:
consolidated items, non-expendable items ( school, gov), expendable items, and finally expendable issues . Now let me explain each item more clearly:

consolidated item:

Basically contains the consolidated/combined list of properties, with following data values:
property head (name of the item), appendix (category name), fund details (details about the fund),
Prev Year value (Total last year value of all the non-expendable items under this category name),
Prev Year Purchases (Total last year purchases of all the non-expendable items under this category name),
UNSV value (Total unservicable value of all the non-expendable items under this category),
SERV value (Total servicable value of all the non-expendable items under this category),
Dep value (Total depreciated value of all the non-expendable items under this category),

Present value (Total value of all the non-expendable items under this category at present) (Not stored in db, computed when needed),

Non Expendable Items:

Basically contains every items present under each consolidated item category, with following data values:
Ser no (serial number of the item),
lp no (assigned number to the item),
nomenclature (Name of the item),
nonexpendable_type (type of non expendable either 'gov', 'sch')
a_u (basically tells about the unit of the item like if its pieces , mtrs, set, box etc)
vintage (when was this item bought)
qty (Total qty of the item)
rate (Rate of the item)
qty_ledger (total ledger qty of the item)
qty_ground (total ground qunatity of the item)
qty_serv (Total servicable quantity of the item)
qty_unsv (Total unservicable quantity of the item)
dep_value (Percentage of depriciation basically stores values like if 12% -> 12 14.56% -> 14.56)
remarks: there are threee types of remarks that could be added ( auction, destroy or used-up) followed by the qty that is auctioned , destroyed or used-up

Last year bid value (computed using qty and rate) (not stored in db , computed when needed),
serv_value (computed using rate and qty_serv) (not stored in db , computed when needed),
unsv_value (computed using rate and qty_unsv)  (not stored in db , computed when needed),
dep price (final price after depriciation computed using dep_value , rate and qty_serv),


Expendable Items:

ser no (Serial no of the item)
items (name of the item)
unit (similar as a_u)
Qty Prev Month (Total quantity left over after last month)
Purchase curr month (Total qty purchased this month)
utilized curr month (total qty that is utilized in the current month)
remarks (optional field to store any remarks)

final balance (Computed using Lat month quantity, purchase curr month and utilized curr month) ( not stored in db , computed when needed)


Expendable items issue:

ser no (Serial no of the issue)
date ( date the item was issued or purchased from a vendor )
category_id (Expendable item category based on the ser no)
rv_iv ( name of the vendor that sold a particular qty item to us)
whom_id_issue (name of the person to whim a particular qty of item was issued to)
qty_received (Total qty received from the vendor)
qty_issued (Total qty issued to the person)
